Camera

Start the camera, frame the whole wall with the printed marker stuck on it, then press Auto-calibrate.

Wall calibration - size is measured automatically

1. Stick the printed EchoVein marker flat on the wall (python make_marker.py), fully visible. 2. Press Auto-calibrate: the marker and wall edges are found and the wall's width and height are calculated for you. 3. If the edges are not found (or you only want one part of the wall), tick "select the area myself", drag the 4 numbered corners around it and press "Measure & calibrate" - that area's width/height is calculated too. No typing of sizes needed.
Advanced: type dimensions manually (only if you cannot use a marker)
